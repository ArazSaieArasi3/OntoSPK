# Documentation maintenance

Use descriptive scientific and technical titles. Internal issue, workstream and execution identifiers belong in provenance records when needed. Reference pages, explanatory pages, tutorials and evaluation reports may use different structures suited to their tasks.

Definitions and logical commitments are governed by the model and explicit profile. Editing should preserve claim strength, limitations, negative results and version identity. Do not describe an unexecuted test as passing or a prepared site as deployed.

Curated source is in `docs/guide`; Wiki and portal pages are projections. Correct the source and regenerate projections. External citations, tool attribution and necessary provenance remain available.

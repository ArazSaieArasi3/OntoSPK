Limitations and open scientific questions

This is the preserved core specification or guide baseline. Ontology core v2.0.0 is unchanged; the accepted bounded FOL evidence is v2.0.6. Historical preparation labels and earlier test counts below retain their original context. Read the current formal verification method and version explanation and the evaluation overview. Reviewed diagrams and domains · Citation and evidence handoff · Current capability profile decision.

The public v2.0.0 ontology-core release has bounded synthetic evaluation. It does not claim:

  • independent expert/domain acceptance of the complete conceptual model;
  • acceptance or exact-class equivalence by external ontology owners;
  • complete UFO modal, counterfactual or causal semantics;
  • end-to-end identity continuity enforced by the separate transition policy;
  • complete implementation of all upstream validator rules;
  • population coverage of every local class or association;
  • production deployment or empirical industrial effectiveness;
  • a successor PostgreSQL implementation;
  • a DOI or a deployed site merely because source files exist.

The current identity and relation decisions remain inspectable, including unresolved scientific boundaries. Negative and partial results are part of the evidence. A passing reasoner check establishes only its documented logical scope; a passing data-admission fixture establishes only its documented finite-data behavior.

The complete external-reference register distinguishes 16 historically observed same-name declarations, one rename candidate and ten unresolved, undeclared or composite handles. Seventeen preserved class locators use a private research namespace. They are not public download links or accepted owner alignments. Their native fields remain unchanged to preserve the frozen model identity.

WebVOWL is an exploratory OWL view. It does not display every conceptual commitment, SHACL condition or database policy. The separate application Explorer is not part of WebVOWL acceptance.

The related-ontology comparison contains the source-specific literature and official links used for CPM/CPM2, OntoProd, software product-management prior work and software/requirements foundations. Its numbered citations are local to that comparison.

Foundational interpretation draws on the OntoUML identity and category reference, the ROoST testing ontology and the W3C PROV-O specification. These resources inform the local model without establishing exact equivalence or independent acceptance.

Technical execution sources include the pinned OntoUML validator and SDK, ROBOT/HermiT, RDFLib and pySHACL. Their exact execution identities are recorded in Quality and Reproduce. WebVOWL and its dependencies are attributed in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.

Internal source/commit links establish provenance; they are not peer-reviewed literature. Link reachability and scientific relevance are assessed separately.

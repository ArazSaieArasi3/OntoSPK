Domains

This is the preserved core specification or guide baseline. Ontology core v2.0.0 is unchanged; the accepted bounded FOL evidence is v2.0.6. Historical preparation labels and earlier test counts below retain their original context. Read the current formal verification method and version explanation and the evaluation overview. Reviewed diagrams and domains · Citation and evidence handoff · Current capability profile decision.

The domain grouping organizes the native model; it does not define twelve independent OWL imports. Concept ownership is recorded in the class catalogue.

Domain Main concern
Product Portfolio Continuing products, portfolio arrangements and offering specifications.
Product Strategy Strategic direction, objectives and intended value.
Product Definition Product scope and specifications of intended capabilities.
Product Requirement Requirements and their connections to intended product behavior.
Product Experience Intended interactions and experience-related specifications.
Product Roadmap Planned changes, sequencing and planning references.
Product Delivery Delivery specifications and governed delivery records.
Product Test Test specifications, concluded executions and consolidated results.
Product Marketing Positioning, market-facing specifications and related references.
Product Evidence Evidence records and the claims or observations they support.
Product Realization Actual product realizations and realized capabilities.
Product Reference Reference records connecting the local model to externally governed subjects.

These short descriptions are navigation aids. Exact entity definitions, stereotypes, identity commitments and relation contracts are in the class catalogue and association catalogue.

The selected conceptual view displays forty selected concepts. It is not the full model; the complete native source retains all 128 conceptual classes, including 27 external handles.

Evaluation and evidence

OntoSPK publishes finite test outcomes and their limitations. The core release records ten integration stages, including 374 exact-answer comparisons across 56 competency contracts, and 13 HermiT scenarios. These overlapping denominators are not a semantic-correctness percentage.

Post-release component evaluation

The subsequent evaluation programme has a finite register. The graph audit covers four active SHACL graphs; isolated simple-component cases cover 1,864 occurrences, with 1,754 constructible expected violations. Complex interactions and expanded OWL evaluation remain open. The component cases are not an end-to-end conformance claim.

Publication acceptance

The Wiki receipt records a separate publication check. Browser and deployment receipts for Pages must identify their own exact source.

Limits

Independent domain review, external-owner equivalence, modal and causal adequacy, and production readiness are not established by these finite checks. Read the limitations before reuse.

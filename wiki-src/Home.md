# OntoSPK documentation

OntoSPK describes software product knowledge across intended specifications, actual realizations and the evidence used to interpret them. This guide explains the selected public ontology core; the model and its recorded evidence remain the sources for exact definitions and test outcomes.

## Understand the model

Read the [Overview](Overview), [Domains](Domains) and [Conceptual Model](Conceptual-Model). The [External References](External-References) page explains which boundary links remain unresolved. [Formalization](Formalization) and [Constraints](Constraints) distinguish conceptual commitments, OWL meaning and finite data validation.

## Examine the evidence

[Quality](Quality) states what the finite checks establish. [Competency Questions](Competency-Questions) identifies the query contracts; [Reproduce](Reproduce) gives executable instructions. Read [Limitations](Limitations) before interpreting results beyond their tested scope.

## Explore and cite

[Visualization](Visualization) explains the selected diagram and WebVOWL view. [Versions](Versions), [References](References) and [Citation](Citation) identify the release and its supporting sources. For exhaustive lookup, use the generated [native reference](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/OntoSPK/blob/v2.0.0/docs/reference/native/index.html) and [OWL declaration reference](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/OntoSPK/blob/v2.0.0/docs/reference/owl/index.html) from the released source. The references are also available through the published Pages portal; current and frozen documentation have separate source identities.

## Concept explanations and sources

The [source-backed concept reader](https://arazsaiearasi3.github.io/OntoSPK/current/concepts/index.html) provides a categorized index of all 128 native entries and six editorial pilot pages, with canonical definitions, original explanations, examples and claim-specific IEEE-style references. Five local concepts and one external boundary have manuscripts; 122 entries await editorial coverage. [Read the sourcing and maintenance method](https://arazsaiearasi3.github.io/OntoSPK/current/concepts/method.html).

# OntoSPK documentation

OntoSPK describes software product knowledge across intended specifications, actual realizations and the evidence used to interpret them. This guide explains the selected public ontology core; the model and its recorded evidence remain the sources for exact definitions and test outcomes.

## Understand the model

Read the [Overview](Overview), [Domains](Domains) and [Conceptual Model](Conceptual-Model). The [External References](External-References) page explains which boundary links remain unresolved. [Formalization](Formalization) and [Constraints](Constraints) distinguish conceptual commitments, OWL meaning and finite data validation.

## Examine the evidence

[Quality](Quality) states what the finite checks establish. [Competency Questions](Competency-Questions) identifies the query contracts; [Reproduce](Reproduce) gives executable instructions. Read [Limitations](Limitations) before interpreting results beyond their tested scope.

[Visualization](Visualization) explains the selected diagram and WebVOWL view. [Versions](Versions), [References](References) and [Citation](Citation) identify the release and its supporting sources.

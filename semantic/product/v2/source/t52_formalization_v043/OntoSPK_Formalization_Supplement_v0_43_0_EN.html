Result: all 13 fresh OWL 2 DL profile checks passed. HermiT matched all 13 expected outcomes: eight consistent inputs and five deliberately inconsistent controls. The native structural register retains 892 matched expressions; it is not a count of independent OWL axioms.

Interpretation and notation

Variables range over a single logical interpretation unless a formula is explicitly designated as an operational policy. exists! combines existence and uniqueness. Native relation formulas use stable native identifiers and follow properties[0] to properties[1]; each endpoint bound constrains objects at that end per opposite endpoint. They are static expressions, not modal or causal UFO commitments.

OWL uses open-world semantics without a unique-name assumption. Missing explicit fillers can be compatible with existential restrictions; two names can denote one individual. The paired distinctness cases use owl:differentFrom. SHACL and SQL assess the supplied finite data and explicit record bindings under their documented configurations. A rule's absence is not a positive claim of real-world falsity.

The current operational profile preserves the retained logical axioms and updates only two explanatory comments about TestExecution and recordsExecution. The 46-triple occurrence fixture is separated into 37 logical triples and nine operational metadata triples for its selected reasoner run; every metadata triple is retained and separately checked by the occurrence profile. No input disappearance is claimed as successful reasoning.
